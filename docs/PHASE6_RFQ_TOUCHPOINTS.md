# Phase 6 RFQ / Quote Touchpoints

Status: ACTIVE FOUNDATION
Date: 2026-09-29

## Human agency
- Buyer explicitly selects suppliers to invite.
- Quote comparison shows factual commercial fields and totals.
- The system does not rank, score, recommend or select a winning supplier.
- Quote acceptance/order conversion is intentionally outside this phase.

## Controlled messaging
- Messages exist only inside an RFQ context.
- Only the owning business, invited suppliers and authorized platform operators may access the thread.
- Message mutations are audited.
- Direct contact details are not required for the workflow.

## Help
Help & Site Guide must explain RFQ creation, requested quantities, supplier invitations, supplier quote submission, tax/price fields, validity dates, comparison and message scope.

## Privacy / commercial confidentiality
- RFQ lines, quotes and messages are commercially sensitive and tenant-scoped.
- Quote data from one supplier must not be exposed to another supplier.
- Retention/export rules must preserve required commercial evidence while minimizing unnecessary access.
- Later order conversion must snapshot the explicitly chosen commercial terms.

Production remains locked.
