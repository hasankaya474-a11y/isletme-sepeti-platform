# Phase 11 Finance Touchpoints
Status: ACTIVE FOUNDATION
Date: 2026-09-29

This is an engineering control map, not accounting, tax or legal advice.

- Financial ledger entries are append-only.
- Corrections are explicit reversal or adjustment entries.
- Money is stored in integer minor units plus currency.
- Reconciliation differences remain visible until explicitly resolved or dismissed.
- Commission rules are configuration records and require human-approved activation.
- No payment execution, settlement instruction, bank transfer, card charge or wallet movement exists in this phase.
- Financial and reconciliation access is permission scoped and audited.
- Help content must explain debit/credit direction, reversal behavior, commission basis and reconciliation resolution.
- Production remains locked.
